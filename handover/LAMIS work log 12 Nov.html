What changed in the app today, compared with the version you last saw. The drawings are simple sketches of each change, not screenshots.

Maps

Card pulls up over the map

Pulling the activity card up takes it to the search bar and covers the map. While it is up, the search bar only filters that list; the map stays where it is. Clearing the search with the x, or pulling the card down, lowers the keyboard.

HERE filter: calendar for the day

The row of upcoming dates is replaced by a calendar, from today to one year ahead. Tap a day to see only that day. "All days" clears it. It is hidden while you search.

Pin card: "How far?" removed, no flash

The "How far?" button is gone from the card that opens when you tap a pin. The flicker when you tap the empty map is fixed: the card keeps the pin's activities while it slides down, instead of switching to the full list too early.

"Near me" names your town

In Chalakudy it now says Chalakudy. The lookup uses a closer zoom and takes the town name first. Needs an API restart and a deploy on the live site.

HERE map search

Search by place name, activity name or category (coffee, yoga, sports). Results arrive 20 at a time, with "Show all results", and the server does a light query.

Settings and look

Text size: Compact, Medium, Large

Three sizes instead of five. Compact is the smallest and the default; Medium and Large are one step up each. Anyone who had picked a size keeps the same look under the new name. The "Send a test push" row is removed from Settings.

THERE profile colour

The THERE profile header now fades from white at the top to sand behind the stats card, as in the design.

Chat

Only the white input box grows with the text and fits it tightly, then shrinks back after sending. The unread count updates correctly when you open a chat.

Create, sign-up and fixes

  • Time picker: the hour wheel flips AM/PM (PM goes to AM, AM goes to PM). Create's place step shows a map preview. Durations 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 hours.
  • Sign-up: password length hint, control characters refused, "email already used" message. Onboarding swipes.
  • Android calendar: Saturday was missing in the date picker, fixed.
  • Counts: Following and Followers numbers now match their lists.
  • Trip review: white cards, summary and dates cards, no dark colour.

Still to come: the same pin-card fix on the THERE map, and Italian text for the new labels.
