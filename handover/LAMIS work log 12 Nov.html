What changed

  • Text size: Compact (default), Medium and Large. "Send a test push" removed from Settings.
  • Look: smaller text, cards, pills and photos; slimmer Home header; "Leaving soon" and "Next up" as separate cards; default profile picture dark green with white initials; bottom bar hides while scrolling.
  • Trips and activities: smaller faces in "Who's going"; trip review with white cards and summary; AM/PM switch in the time picker; map preview in Create; Saturday shown in the Android date picker.
  • Maps: search by place, activity or category; activity card pulls up to the search bar; calendar in the HERE filter; "How far?" removed; no flicker on the pin card; "Near me" shows your town.
  • Profile and chat: THERE profile header fades from white to sand; message box grows with the text; Following and Followers numbers match their lists.
  • Sign-up: password length hint, email already used message, swipeable welcome screens.
