LAMIS

UI component library

10 November 2026

One list of the colours, type, spacing and components that the LAMIS app is built from, taken from the app as it runs today. HERE and THERE share every component; only the colour set changes. This is the reference for the Figma component specifications, which are produced separately.

Rule of the system: one component, one look. A screen uses a shared component and never its own version of a button, card, chip, field, empty state or loading block.

1. Brand

ElementSpecification
Primary wordmark"lamis" with the sage dot. Light version (white letters, sage dot #9CB397) on dark backgrounds, dark version (deep green #082F27, dot #859B7A) on light backgrounds. Top-left of the home pages and Inbox, about 28 pt tall.
Dot mark (app icon)The "l" with the sage dot. Dark icon: ivory mark on #0B322A. Used as the Android notification icon and the launch screen.
Tagline"Be here. Go there." The only tagline, in headers and the welcome screen.
HERE and THERETwo experiences inside LAMIS, chosen with one switch on Home. HERE is green, THERE is warm beige.

3. Typography

RoleSpecification
TypefaceInter on iPhone and Android, four weights: Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600, Bold 700. Heavier requests use Bold. Icons use one outline icon set.
Page title24–26 pt, SemiBold, line height 30–32.
Section title17–20 pt, SemiBold or Bold.
Body and controls14–16 pt, Regular or Medium, line height about 1.3–1.4.
Small and labels11–13 pt, muted colour. Status chips 11–12 pt Bold.
Letter spacingOnly the HERE / THERE switch words use wide spacing (1.2). Everything else is normal.
Text sizeCompact, Standard, Large, Larger in Settings; everything scales together. Phones narrower than 390 pt scale down slightly (the chat a little more).

4. Shape and spacing

TokenValue
Page gutter16 pt (24 pt on large screens). Gap between blocks 14–16.
RadiusButtons 12 · fields 11 (form pills 32–40) · cards 14 (feed cards 24–30) · chips and pills fully round · bottom sheets 22 · the page sheet over the header band 40 · dashed empty state 16.
Touch targetAt least 44 pt (buttons are 42 tall with extra touch area; small buttons 34 with touch area).
Card1 pt line, 12–16 pt padding, no heavy shadow.
Safe areasThe bottom home bar and gesture bar are always respected; pages scroll to their last item with room under it.

5. Components

GroupComponent and rule
NavigationDock: five tabs (Home, Search or Map, raised Create, Inbox, Profile), same in HERE and THERE; hidden on Create and Profile and while scrolling down Home. HERE | THERE switch: one pill, Home only. Header band: forest in HERE, sand in THERE, the page rises over it as a rounded sheet; on Home it collapses into a small row. Back button: round, 44 pt, centred title, on every inner page.
ButtonsPrimary (forest), secondary (white, line), accent, danger (outlined), ghost (text only), off (grey, "not ready yet"). One size and a small size. A full-width primary button ends every form.
InputsField (label above, placeholder far lighter than text, disappears when you tap), search bar, pill input for the create forms, checkbox row, counter (− number +), time wheel (own build for iPhone and Android), calendar (day or date range; days before the first allowed day are greyed). A field in error: 1.2 pt red border and one line saying what is missing.
CardsActivity card (big, row, compact) and trip card (big, row, compact): picture, title, place and time, spots left, faces of who is going, SAMPLE tag for demo content. Profile card, stats card, account row (icon, title, note, chevron, optional red dot).
Chips and labelsStatus chip (ok, warning, problem, info). Category and travel-interest chip: round photo plus name, selected = forest. Travel style label: "Travel together" (forest, white text, people icon) and "Meet at destination" (sand, forest text, pin icon). Warning / Appealed / Waiting for decision / Accepted / Rejected chips. SAMPLE tag. New dot.
FeedbackEmpty state: soft icon, title, one explaining line, one button. Skeleton: grey blocks in the shape of the real rows (list rows, report rows, detail page, chat). No spinners. Toast, banner (one slim line), pop-up in the app's own style on Android.
SheetsBottom sheet, edit pop-up that grows out of its card on the review pages, date sheet that floats above the bottom edge.
Create flowStep header (title, "Step n/4", four progress bars), review card with an Edit pill, red border and one line when its part is missing.

6. Motion

  • HERE / THERE switch: a short colour fade with the experience's logo, about a third of a second.
  • Home: the white sheet rises and the header shrinks with the scroll, in order (city and tagline fade first, then the logo and photo). The dock slides away on the way down and back on the way up.
  • Pull to refresh: the sheet stays still, the content moves down, the spinner shows at the top of the sheet.
  • Haptic clicks only on iPhone; none on Android.

7. Accessibility

  • Every control has a spoken label; selected, disabled and loading states are announced.
  • Text is never a picture; contrast follows the colour pairs above.
  • Pages work at the largest text size and scroll to the last element.
