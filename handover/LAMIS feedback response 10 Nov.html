It also stays hidden when you open Settings from Profile and come back. Every other screen leaves room under its last item, so you can scroll to the very end. On Home the bar slides away while you scroll down and returns when you scroll up. It is the same bar in HERE and THERE. iPhone home bar and Android gesture bar are respected. Checked at 390 pt and 360 pt wide; real devices are still to be tested.

Switching takes about a third of a second (it took about three seconds before). The choice is remembered across Home, Search, Create, Inbox and Profile, and after closing the app. Opening an alert or link that belongs to the other experience switches for you. HERE and THERE share one account and one profile; switching creates nothing.

One set of shared components for buttons, cards, chips, fields, empty states and loading placeholders (see the separate UI component library). Checklist: category and name ✓ · description ✓ · location (a place searched and picked in your city, with a "public place" confirmation) ✓ · date, start time and duration, with the end time shown ✓ · maximum participants ✓ · participation condition (join at once or request to join) ✓ · final confirmation ✓. Not in the app: paid activities (the app takes no payments), and a separate meeting-instructions text (section 3).
6Trip creation (THERE)Done Your question: is "Travel together" versus "Meet at destination" supported? It was not, and it is now. The first choice on the trip form is "How will the group get there?". Travel together asks for a departure city. Meet at destination has no departure city and the trip is for people travelling on their own. It shows on trip cards (a green or sand label on the picture), on the trip page, in the review and in the share text, and it is a filter in search.
Departure and destination are separate fields and are only set by searching and picking a place. Return before departure is refused with a message. The date icon sits inside each date field. The review page lets you edit before publishing, and it checks everything again.
Additional development still open: for "Travel together", an applicant cannot yet say they leave from a different city than the organiser (section 3).

  • Brand: LAMIS wordmark in the headers, tagline fixed, app named LAMIS, dot icon, Android launch screen.

5. Clickable prototype: how to see each flow

The running app is the clickable prototype, and every step below can be followed on a phone build or in the browser preview. Sample sign-in details are provided separately. In the test version, sign-up codes appear in the app and identity verification is simulated, so you choose the result.

FlowSteps to follow
RegistrationWelcome → Sign up → enter details and confirm you are 18+ → enter the code shown in the app → short profile step → Home.
HERE discovery and participationHome (HERE) → choose a category chip or search → open an activity → "Request to join" or "Join now" → host approves → "Open group chat". Without verification the button reads "Get verified to join".
Activity creationCreate → four steps (what, when, where, who) → Review → edit any part → "Publish activity". Leaving the form keeps it as a draft.
THERE discoverySwitch to THERE → interest chips or Search (opens on All trips) → Travel together / Meet at destination → open a trip → apply.
Trip creationCreate (in THERE) → pick Travel together or Meet at destination → search and pick places → dates → budget and interests → guidelines → Review → "Publish trip".
Profile navigationProfile → Settings (language, text size, notifications) → Reports & Blocked → Friends and followers → My activities, My trips, Favourites.
VerificationAny "Get verified" button, or Settings → Identity verification → read how it works → agree → choose a method → simulated result → blue check on the profile.
MessagingInbox → Chats (conversations) and Alerts (notifications) → open a group chat → write a message; trip hosts can add a date poll.
