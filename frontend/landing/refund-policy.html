Refund & Cancellation Policy

Payments, refunds and cancellations on LAMIS.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

LAMIS is free

LAMIS is free to download and use. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or fees, so there is nothing to refund.

Money between members

The app never takes, holds or passes on money. Costs shared on a plan or trip (such as a coffee, tickets or accommodation) are arranged directly between members, outside the app. CloudHouse Technologies Pvt. Ltd. cannot refund or recover them; settle them directly with the people involved.

Cancelling a plan or trip

Hosts can cancel a plan or trip in the app, and everyone in it is told. Members can leave a plan; leaving at the last minute can affect their reliability level.

If this changes

If we ever add paid features, we will publish the refund terms on this page before they apply.
