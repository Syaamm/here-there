What LAMIS does and does not promise.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

A platform, not an organiser

LAMIS lets members find and organise plans and trips themselves. CloudHouse Technologies Pvt. Ltd. does not run, supervise or attend them, and is not a travel agent, tour operator or booking service.

Verification is not a background check

A verified badge means a member passed an identity check confirming they are a real adult. It does not mean they are safe or trustworthy. Use your own judgement, meet in public places and report anything that worries you.

Information from members

Plans, trips, profiles, budgets and messages are written by members. We do not check them for accuracy. Trip budgets are personal estimates, not quotes or offers.

Reliability levels

Levels show how dependably someone has turned up in the past. They are not a safety rating or a judgement of character.

Maps and places

Venues, distances and maps come from third-party map services and may be approximate or out of date.

No payments

The app does not process payments. Any money arranged between members is entirely their own responsibility.
