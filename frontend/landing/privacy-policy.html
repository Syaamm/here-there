Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information when you use LAMIS.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Who we are

LAMIS is an app provided by CloudHouse Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us"). It has two modes: HERE, for finding and hosting small plans happening in the next few hours, and THERE, for finding people to travel with. You have one account for both.

You can reach us at +91 480 2737360 or support@cloudhousetechnologies.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Your account: email address, password (we keep only a scrambled form), name, city, short bio, languages, interests and an optional profile photo. Photos are re-encoded, so no camera or location details stay in them.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google (optional): if you choose it, Apple or Google confirms who you are to us. We keep only your account ID with that provider and the email address it confirms (with Apple's “Hide My Email”, the private relay address Apple gives us). If the provider shares your name we use it as your display name, which you can change. We never receive your Apple or Google password, and we do not read your contacts, calendar or anything else in those accounts. You can still set a password later with “Forgot password”.
  • Phone number (optional): only if you add it. It is stored encrypted, and one number can belong to one account.
  • Identity check: to keep in-person meetups safer, only verified adults can join, host or chat. We keep only the result (verified or not), the type of check, when it expires and when you agreed to it. We never keep your ID document photo, selfie or face data. If a specialist identity provider is used, it is asked to erase its copy once the result is in.
  • What you do in the app: plans and trips you create or ask to join, your short notes to hosts, group chat messages, people you follow or block (private to you), favourites, drafts and alerts.
  • Reliability level: a simple level (New, Reliable, Very reliable or Needs care) worked out from finished plans and trips, confirmed no-show notes, late exits and reports a moderator upheld. You can see it, dispute any no-show note, and see the full breakdown in your data download.
  • Location: we do not track you. If you tap "Near me" or "How far?", your phone sends an approximate position (rounded to about 1 km) for that one request. We do not save it.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a push token from your phone so we can send you alerts.
  • Safety and security: reports about content or behaviour and what our team did about them, a scrambled fingerprint of a random device code (to limit fake accounts and stop banned people starting again), and server logs of requests, used only to keep the service secure and working.

We do not collect your contacts or microphone data, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account and show your profile to the people you do plans and trips with.
  • To check that members are real adults before they meet in person.
  • To order your feed by simple signals: how soon a plan starts, places left, your interests, people you follow, distance (only if you share your position) and how new it is. It never hides plans from you.
  • To send you alerts about your plans, trips and chats.
  • To detect fraud and abuse, handle reports and keep members safe.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show advertising or track you across other apps. The app never handles money between members.

5. Who we share it with

Other members see what is needed to meet: your name, photo, city, bio, languages, verified badge and reliability level. We also use service providers that work for us under contract and only to do that job: hosting, email and SMS delivery, the identity-check provider, push notification services (Apple, Google Firebase and Expo), map and place-search services, and error tracking. We may share information if the law requires it or to protect someone from harm.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account and as long as it is needed for the purposes in section 3. We do not currently delete older records on a fixed schedule. In practice:

  • Your account details, photos, messages, alerts, drafts, travel-matching wishes, warnings, review requests and identity-check results are erased when you delete your account. Of the identity check we only keep the outcome, never your documents or your face.
  • Group chat and private travel-match chat messages stay while your account exists, so people can look back at what was agreed. Messages that someone has reported may be kept after you delete your account, for safety reasons.
  • Safety reports, records of attendance and reliability, and records of staff actions may be kept after you delete your account, for as long as needed to protect members and to handle legal claims. They do not show your name or contact details once your account is deleted.
  • Plans and trips stay as history for the other people in them, with your account shown as "Deleted user".
  • Sign-in and confirmation codes stop working soon after they are sent.

If we introduce fixed deletion periods, we will update this page and the app first. The current list is always in the app under Settings > Your data.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are scrambled, phone numbers are encrypted, staff accounts use two-step sign-in, and staff actions are recorded. No service is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can, at any time and for free:

  • Download your data: Settings > Your data > Download my data (ZIP).
  • Correct it: Edit profile, Change email, Change password.
  • Delete your account: Settings > Your data > Delete my account. See Account Deletion.
  • Withdraw consent, or object to or ask us to restrict some uses, by writing to support@cloudhousetechnologies.com.
  • Complain to a data protection authority. In Italy, that is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

9. Children

LAMIS is only for adults aged 18 and over. People under 18 cannot be verified or use it to meet.

10. Changes

When this policy changes we update this page and its date. If a change matters, we tell you in the app before it applies.
