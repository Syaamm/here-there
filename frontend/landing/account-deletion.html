Account Deletion

How to delete your LAMIS account and data.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open LAMIS and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings > Your data.
  3. Optional: tap Download my data (ZIP) to keep a copy first.
  4. Tap Delete my account, enter your password and type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Can't open the app?

Write to support@cloudhousetechnologies.com from the email address on your account, or call +91 480 2737360. We verify the request before acting.

What is deleted

  • Your name, photo, bio, email, phone number and sign-in details.
  • Your messages, favourites, follows, blocks, drafts, travel-matching wishes and identity-check details.
  • Plans you host that have not finished are cancelled, and the people in them are told.

What we may keep

Past plans and trips stay in other members' history, with you shown as "Deleted user". Messages someone reported and our team's safety records may be kept for as long as needed to protect people and handle legal claims (up to 2 years), then deleted.

Timeline

Deletion in the app is immediate. Requests sent to us are completed within 30 days of verification.
