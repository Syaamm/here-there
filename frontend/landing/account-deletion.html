Account Deletion

How to delete your LAMIS account and data.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open LAMIS and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings > Your data.
  3. Optional: tap Download my data (ZIP) to keep a copy first.
  4. Tap Delete my account…, enter your password, type DELETE and tap Delete my account to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out. We send a short confirmation to your email address. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Can't open the app?

If you cannot open the app, or your account is suspended (a suspended account cannot be deleted from the app), write to support@cloudhousetechnologies.com from the email address on your account, or call +91 480 2737360. We verify the request before acting.

What is deleted

  • Your name, photos, bio, email, phone number, sign-in details and linked social sign-ins. Your email address is replaced and your password can no longer be used.
  • Your messages, alerts, push-notification settings, favourites, follows, blocks, drafts, travel-matching wishes, warnings, review requests and identity-check results. A check still open with our identity provider is erased there too.
  • Plans and trips you host that have not finished are cancelled, and you are removed from the ones you joined. The people in them are told.

What we may keep

Past plans and trips stay in other members' history, with you shown as "Deleted user". Messages someone reported, and our team's safety and audit records, may be kept for as long as needed to protect people and to handle legal claims. They do not show your name or contact details. To ask about a specific record, write to us at the address above.

Timeline

Deletion in the app is immediate. Requests sent to us are completed within 30 days of verification.
