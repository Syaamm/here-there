Sharing

Shared only with the members you meet and with the service providers named in the Privacy Policy. We do not sell data, show ads or track you across other apps.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are scrambled, phone numbers are encrypted, and staff access is limited by role with two-step sign-in.

Your control

Download your data or delete your account at any time in Settings > Your data. See Account Deletion.
