## What this app is
HERE helps you find and host small plans happening in the next few hours. THERE helps you find people to travel with. You have one account for both.

## What we collect, and why
- **Your account:** email, password (we keep only a scrambled form), your name, city, short bio, languages and interests, and an optional photo. We use these to run your account and show your profile to people you do plans with.
- **Identity check:** to keep in-person meetups safer we check that members are real adults. A specialist provider, **[PROVIDER NAME]**, looks at your ID document (and, if you choose the face-match option, a live selfie). **We never receive or keep your ID photo, selfie or face data.** We keep only the result (verified or not), when it expires, which kind of check it was, and when you agreed to it. The provider is asked to erase its copy once the result is in.
- **Your choice of check:** you can choose a check with **no selfie and no face matching**. The face-match option uses biometric data, which is treated with extra care, and we only use it if you give your explicit consent. You can choose the other option instead, and it does not affect how you can use the app afterwards.
- **Your phone number (optional):** only if you add it. We keep it encrypted. One number can belong to one account.
- **What you do in the app:** plans and trips you create or ask to join, your short messages to hosts, group chat messages, people you follow or block (private to you), favourites, and alerts we send you.
- **Drafts:** when you start a plan or trip and leave the form, or leave the app, what you typed is saved to your account as a draft so you can finish it later, also on another phone. Only you can see your drafts, and you can delete them. A form you left empty saves nothing.
- **Searching for a meeting place:** when you search for a place, the words you type and the city are sent through our server to a map service to find it. They are not linked to your account, and we do not keep them. The place you choose is saved with your plan as its public venue.
- **Sharing a plan:** if you share a public plan, the link opens a page anyone can see. It shows only the title, the day and the city, never the venue, who is hosting or who is going.
- **Where you are:** we do **not** track your location. If you tap "Near me" or "How far?", your phone gives an approximate position (rounded to about 1 km) for that one request. We do not save it. Plans show only the public venue.
- **Safety:** reports that people send about content or behaviour, and the actions our team takes, with a record of who did what (names of staff and ids, not your messages).
- **Keeping the service safe:** a random code the app makes for your device, kept only as a scrambled fingerprint, to limit how many accounts one device can create and to stop people who were removed from starting again.

- **Reliability:** so that hosts and members can choose who to meet with more confidence, we work out a simple level (New, Reliable, Very reliable or Needs care). It uses only plans and trips you were verified for that have finished, notes from hosts that you did not come (which only count if you do not dispute them within 7 days, or a moderator confirms them), leaving a plan at the last minute, and reports that a moderator upheld. It never uses likes, profile views or whether people got on with you. Verified members can see your level, and so can you (Profile, Reliability). The full list of what it is based on is in your data download (Settings, Your data), and you can dispute any note a host makes about you. Older events count less every month and drop out after 2 years.

## What we do not do
We do not sell your data. We do not show advertising. We do not use your data to build a profile for sale. We do not share your documents with anyone, because we never hold them.

## How we choose what to show you
The home feed is ordered by simple signals: how soon an activity starts, whether people you follow are going, how many places are left, your interests, how close it is (only if you share your position) and how new it is. It never hides plans from you, and you can see everything ordered by time instead. Nobody is refused anything because of this ordering.

## How long we keep things
Group chat messages are kept 90 days after the plan or trip ends. Alerts: 90 days once read, 1 year if never read. Closed safety reports and our activity records: 2 years. Old identity-check results: 1 year after the decision. When you delete your account, your personal details are erased straight away. ## Your rights
You can, at any time and for free: see and **download your data** (Settings, Your data), **correct it** (Edit profile, Change email, Change password), **delete your account** (Settings, Your data, Delete my account), withdraw consent you gave, object to or ask us to restrict some uses (write to us), and complain to the data protection authority. In Italy that is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

When you delete your account we erase your name, photo, bio, email, phone number, messages, favourites, follows and identity details. We may keep messages that someone reported, and the safety records of our team, for as long as needed to protect people and defend legal claims, then delete them.

## Security
Passwords are scrambled, phone numbers are encrypted, staff accounts use two-step sign-in, and we keep a record of staff actions. No service is perfectly secure; we will tell you and the authority if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

## Children
HERE & THERE is for adults aged 18 and over. People who are not 18 cannot be verified or use it to meet.
